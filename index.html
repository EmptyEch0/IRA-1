Chapter 06 — Buyer Guidance

Buyer Guide & Useful FAQs

Essential guidance for prospective land and home buyers in Visakhapatnam. We believe informed investors make the most secure property decisions.

01.

How does IRA Promoters verify property titles?

Before recommending any open plot or residential layout, our legal panel executes a comprehensive 30-year revenue title search, verifies non-encumbrance certificates (EC), scrutinizes master plan zoning, and confirms layout sanctions (VMRDA / competent authority clearances).

02.

Are all recommended plots compliant with Vaastu principles?

Yes. Vaastu compliance is one of our fundamental pillars. Layout alignments prioritize balanced cardinal orientations (East and North-facing plots), proportionate width-to-depth dimensions, and appropriate placement of road intersections to encourage positive energy and long-term peace of mind.

03.

How can I schedule a personal site inspection?

Site inspections can be scheduled every day between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Simply click "Schedule a Site Visit" or WhatsApp us at +91 95739 60563. Guided site tours depart directly from our PM Palem office, accompanied by project executives equipped with title photocopies and layout plans.

04.

What payment plans and registration assistance are provided?

We structure customer-friendly milestone plans pegged to development stages. Upon agreement execution, our administrative team assists with government valuation assessments, stamp duty documentation, and seamless sub-registrar deed registration.
